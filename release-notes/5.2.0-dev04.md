# Training shadow evaluation and reliable grid refresh

## Shadow evaluation

- Evaluate completed training observations separately for charging and discharging using bounded counterfactual scoring.
- Surface proposed gains, confidence, and predicted error/overshoot metrics for review.
- Keep all candidate parameters in shadow state; they are not applied to live regulation.
- Treat the evaluation as an initial screening estimate, not a full device/grid simulation or a guarantee of improved results.

## Grid refresh reliability

- Schedule grid-event-triggered refreshes through Home Assistant's thread-safe job scheduler.
- Avoid cross-thread task creation and the resulting unawaited refresh coroutine warnings.
