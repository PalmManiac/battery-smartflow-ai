# Battery SmartFlow AI 5.1.13

## Improved

- Treat the configured maximum charge power as an AC charging limit on device profiles with a confirmed battery-side charging ceiling. Native PV charging can then contribute separately, while the physical total limit is still respected.
- Clarify the AC charging limit in the setting label and include its configured value in debug samples.
- Retain conservative charge-limit behavior for device profiles whose total battery-side ceiling has not been confirmed.
