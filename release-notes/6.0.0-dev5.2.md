# V6.0.0-dev5.2 – Restore the shadow-allocation sensor and portal card

## Changes

- Keep the live `Schattenverteilung` diagnostic sensor registered. It was incorrectly removed by the legacy cleanup that retires other permanent diagnostic entities.
- Restore the sensor state and its allocation attributes to Home Assistant, allowing the portal to show the shadow proposal under the battery-group card.
- Bump the integration version to `6.0.0-dev5.2`.

## Please test

- After updating and reloading the integration, find the `Schattenverteilung` entity and check its state and attributes.
- In the portal's **Systeme & Gruppen** view, confirm that the shadow-allocation card now appears directly below the battery-group card.
- A single-system group should show the `Einzelgeräte-Gruppe` state; it can verify sensor registration and portal display, but cannot validate multi-system allocation.
- Multi-system users: continue testing charge and discharge proposals, SoC limits, offline/setup/HEMS blockers, and electrical circuit limits as described in Dev5.
- Please report the installed version, the sensor state, screenshots of the portal card, and a BSFAI Debug export if the sensor or card is still missing.
