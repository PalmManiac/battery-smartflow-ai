# Hyper 2000 recovery after cleared faults

This maintenance release restores regulation for the Hyper 2000 when its current fault level confirms that the fault has cleared, even if a stale Cloud `is_error` flag remains set. Other Zendure models and Hyper 2000 states without a valid cleared fault level keep the existing protection behavior.

## Fixes

- Allow a Hyper 2000 with a valid `faultLevel` of `0` to recover from a stale error flag and resume regulation. ([PR #808](https://github.com/PalmManiac/battery-smartflow-ai/pull/808))

Restart Home Assistant after installing the update.
