# V6.0.0-dev4.3 – Safer regulation training and adaptive power steps

## What's new

- Training gain suggestions are limited to changes of at most 5%.
- A candidate now needs two compatible training runs for the same direction,
  consistent improvement in each run, and measured command response, settling,
  and overshoot data.
- Candidates based only on the simplified one-step score remain unavailable;
  that score is still shown as an estimate, not a guarantee of real-world
  improvement.
- Already stored gain candidates without the new validation record, or outside
  the new 5% safety envelope, are no longer activated.
- Automatic regulation now applies smaller maximum power steps close to its
  target and allows larger steps as the deviation grows. Fast load protection
  and the existing device limits remain in effect.

## Please test

- Compare automatic regulation near the grid-power target and during larger
  load changes. Watch whether corrections approach the target more smoothly
  without leaving prolonged import or export.
- Start two compatible training runs for a direction. Confirm that one run
  alone does not offer a candidate and that candidates are only offered when
  both runs show consistent improvement and dynamic response values are
  available.
- If a candidate is available, compare its behavior with the standard device
  profile and share the candidate metrics and relevant logs before relying on
  it for normal operation.
