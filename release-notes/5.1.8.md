# Battery SmartFlow AI 5.1.8

## Fixed

- Separate the Zendure SF800Pro and SF800Pro 2 1,000 W AC input limit from their total battery charge limit. Native PV no longer incorrectly reduces the AC inlet allowance; total battery charging remains limited to 1,440 W with the integrated battery and up to 2,000 W when expansion batteries are configured.
- Explicitly enforce the 2,400 W combined battery charging ceiling on the SolarFlow 2400 AC+ and 2400 Pro. On the 2400 Pro, native PV reduces the remaining AC charging headroom without changing the AC input rating.
