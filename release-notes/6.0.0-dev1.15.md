# Reliable discharge decisions and accurate energy flows

This test build includes the latest discharge and portal fixes. Price-based discharge now takes priority over an active AC charge binding, safe-idle transitions are captured in debug recordings, and the portal uses measured Zendure battery power instead of a configured residual value. Unmeasured charging is no longer counted as battery energy in the portal.

## External field tests requested

- During a high-price period, confirm that price-based discharge can start even if an AC charging binding was active immediately before. Check that charging pauses while discharge is selected and that the decision reason matches the actual battery behavior.
- On a SolarFlow 2400 AC, compare the portal battery bubble with the native Zendure battery charge/discharge sensors during charging and discharging. In particular, check that a charge of around 2.2 kW is not shown as only the PV-minus-grid residual.
- Check that the portal energy flow does not count a requested or commanded charge when no measured battery charging is present.
- If the regulation enters safe idle, capture a debug recording around that transition and confirm the safe-idle cause is present in the exported recording.
- Report the BSFAI version, Home Assistant version, approximate local time, selected transport, portal values, native battery readings, and decision reason. Please remove App Tokens, serial numbers, network addresses, and other private identifiers from screenshots and logs.

We will review external reports for this dev1 test build before starting the next development stage.
