# Review and activate regulation training candidates

- Compare the current regulation with a proposed training candidate using the estimated grid-error score, sample coverage, response events, confidence, and overshoot estimate.
- Keep the estimate clearly marked as a simplified screening result, not a guarantee of real-world improvement or faster response.
- Allow an explicit, confirmed activation of proposed proportional gains, limited to the device and transport used for training; provide a reset to the configured device profile.
- Keep candidates inactive by default and ignore saved candidates when the active device or transport does not match.
