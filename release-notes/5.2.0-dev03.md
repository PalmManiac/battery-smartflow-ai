# Manual regulation training

## Passive training

- Add a manually started, bounded training session that records normal regulation activity without creating artificial load changes.
- Keep charging and discharging observations separate and scope saved sessions to the selected device, transport, model, and firmware context.
- Persist completed sessions locally in Home Assistant and expose training activity, sample count, and saved-session diagnostics.
- Training remains observational: it does not change active regulation parameters or issue additional device commands.
