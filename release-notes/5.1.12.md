# Battery SmartFlow AI 5.1.12

## Added

- Add an estimated “battery empty at” timestamp based on Zendure's remaining-output-time telemetry. It is available while the battery is discharging and follows the device-reported estimate.

## Improved

- Allow native Zendure setups to omit a separate PV power entity and use the PV power reported by the device instead.
- Keep optional Zendure diagnostic entities hidden until their transport provides data.

## Fixed

- Clarify that the battery-pack temperature value is the maximum cell temperature.
