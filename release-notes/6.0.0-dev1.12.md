# Portal display of battery serial suffixes

This test build fixes the portal hardware overview so the final four alphanumeric characters of a battery pack's serial number appear beside its pack number. The suffix was already available to Home Assistant device names but was dropped while preparing the portal topology data. The portal receives only the shortened suffix.

## External field tests requested

- Confirm battery pack cards in the portal show both the pack number and the final four serial characters without opening the details.
- Compare the suffix shown in the portal with the corresponding battery-pack device name in Home Assistant.
- For installations with many packs, confirm the labels make each pack easy to identify and report any duplicate suffixes.
- Confirm packs without a usable serial number remain visible with their existing numbered labels.
- After restarting Home Assistant or reloading the integration, confirm the suffix remains visible and pack sensors continue updating.
- Report the exact BSFAI and Home Assistant versions, hub model, pack count, and any missing or duplicate suffixes. Sanitize screenshots and diagnostics so full serial numbers, credentials, and private details are not exposed.

We will review external reports before starting the next development stage.
