# More reliable Zendure standby handling

This maintenance release improves the SF2400 AC transition into standby.

## Fixes

- Keep Smart Mode 1 after a zero-power stop command until both reported power setpoints have remained at zero for 60 seconds. A new charge or discharge target cancels the pending standby transition. This prevents brief zero-power intervals during regulation changes from triggering an unnecessary persistent Smart Mode 0 switch. ([Issue #776](https://github.com/PalmManiac/battery-smartflow-ai/issues/776))

## After updating

Restart Home Assistant after installing the integration.
